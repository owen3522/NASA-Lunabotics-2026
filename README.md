<h1>NASA Lunabotics Challenge 2026</h1>

<h2>About the Challenge</h2>
<b>Lunabotics provides accredited institutions of higher learning students an opportunity to apply the NASA systems engineering process to design and build a prototype Lunar construction robot.</b>

<h2>My Role</h2>

- <b>Co-Lead Engineer</b>

<h2>Software and Skills</h2>

- <b>SOLIDWORKS</b>
- <b>Ansys Mechanical</b>
- <b>Machining</b>
- <b>Mill</b>
- <b>Lathe</b>

<h2>Design Decisions</h2>

- <b>Based on the prior years experimental data we decided to keep: </b> 
- <b>Chassis Frame </b>
- <b>Wheels </b>
- <b>Rocker Arm Suspension </b>
- <b>New this year: </b>
- <b>Fixed Angle Trencher and Linear Actuator System</b>
- <b>Reduced Weight Drivetrain</b>
- <b>Electronics and Autonomy Overhaul</b>

<h2>Competition Results</h2>

- <b>15th overall out of 50+ teams selected by NASA </b> 
- <b>4th Place in Autonomous Operations </b>
